Videogram

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 July 2026

Videogram turns your videos into frame grids, clips, GIFs and more — and it does all of it on your device. It has no accounts, no servers, and no analytics. Your videos never leave your phone through us.

In short  No accounts. No tracking. No analytics. No advertising. We don’t collect your videos, photos, or any personal information — because we have nowhere to send them.

What Videogram works with

Videogram works with videos, Live Photos, and images that you choose from your photo library or files. All processing — extracting frames, detecting faces (via Apple’s on-device Vision framework), building grids, trimming, and exporting videos, GIFs, PNG sequences and audio — happens entirely on your device using Apple’s AVFoundation and Vision frameworks. None of it is uploaded anywhere.

What is stored

Videogram keeps only what’s needed to work: temporary files it creates while processing, and the finished exports you explicitly save to your Photos library or Files. These live on your device (and in your own iCloud only if you choose to save them there). The developer has no access to any of it.

What we do not collect

  • No account, sign-in, name, email, or phone number is required or collected.
  • No analytics, usage tracking, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs of any kind.
  • No location data.
  • Your videos, photos, frames, and exports are never sent to the developer or any third‑party server.

Photo library access

When you pick media, iOS may ask for permission to access your photo library. Videogram only reads the specific items you select, and only writes back files you ask it to save. It never scans or uploads your library.

Subscriptions & purchases

Videogram offers an optional Premium upgrade (monthly, annual, or one‑time lifetime). Purchases are processed by Apple and managed through RevenueCat, which handles subscription status and receipt validation. Videogram identifies your purchase using an anonymous identifier generated per installation — no personal information (name, email, etc.) is sent. Apple’s standard purchase terms and privacy practices apply to the transaction itself.

Children

Videogram is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date above.
